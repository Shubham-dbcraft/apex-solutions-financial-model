# Apex Solutions Inc. – 3-Statement Financial Modeling & Financial Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents a 5-year integrated 3-statement financial model for Apex Solutions Inc., a technology company operating across Software, Hardware and IT Services.

The model was developed as a self-directed financial modeling and analysis case study to apply financial statement analysis, forecasting and Excel-based financial modeling concepts in a practical setting.

## Objectives

- Build an integrated Income Statement, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Statement
- Forecast financial performance for five years
- Develop supporting schedules for working capital, CapEx & depreciation, and debt
- Integrate supporting schedules with the three financial statements
- Analyze profitability, liquidity, leverage and operating efficiency
- Derive investment-oriented insights from the forecast financials

## Model Structure

The financial model includes:

- Income Statement
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow Statement
- Revenue Forecasting
- Working Capital Schedule
- CapEx & Depreciation Schedule
- Debt & Interest Schedule
- Financial Ratio Analysis

## Key Modeling Areas

### Revenue Forecasting

Revenue is forecast across three operating segments:

- Software
- Hardware
- IT Services

### Working Capital

The model incorporates operating assumptions for:

- Accounts Receivable
- Inventory
- Accounts Payable
- Cash Conversion Cycle

### CapEx & Depreciation

A dedicated schedule forecasts capital expenditure and depreciation and links the resulting net PP&E balances to the Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Statement.

### Debt & Interest

The debt schedule models:

- Beginning debt balance
- New debt issuance
- Scheduled debt repayment
- Ending debt balance
- Interest expense
- Interest income

## Key Forecast Results

Based on the completed model:

| Metric | 2023A | 2028E |
|---|---:|---:|
| Revenue | $38,800m | $90,976m |
| EBITDA | $13,700m | $32,404m |
| Net Income | $7,950m | $21,756m |
| Current Ratio | 2.67x | 5.94x |
| Debt / Equity | 1.91x | 0.22x |

### CAGR (2023A–2028E)

- Revenue: **18.6%**
- EBITDA: **18.8%**
- Net Income: **22.3%**

## Financial Analysis

The forecast indicates strong revenue and earnings growth accompanied by improving liquidity and significant deleveraging.

Key observations include:

- Revenue and EBITDA grow at approximately 19% CAGR over the forecast period.
- Net income grows faster than revenue, resulting in a 22.3% CAGR.
- Liquidity strengthens materially, with the current ratio reaching 5.94x by 2028E.
- Debt-to-equity declines from 1.91x to 0.22x as debt is repaid and retained earnings increase.
- Strong operating cash generation allows the company to fund significant CapEx while continuing to reduce debt.
- ROE declines over the forecast period as the equity base grows significantly through retained earnings.
